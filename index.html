Instructions for Adding Your Signature

Windows – Outlook: After copying, go to File › Options › Mail › Signatures, create a new signature, and paste. Use “Paste Special › Merge Formatting” if needed.

Mac – Outlook & Mail App: Copy and paste into the signature settings.

iOS – Mail App: Copy, then go to Settings › Mail › Signature and paste.
