Instructions for Adding Your Signature

Windows – Outlook: Copy, then in Outlook go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Create a new signature and paste. Use “Paste Special” → Merge Formatting to preserve green text.

Mac – Outlook & Mail App: Copy, open your signature settings, create or edit a signature, and paste.

iOS – Mail App: Copy, then on your device go to Settings → Mail → Signature. Paste into the signature field.
