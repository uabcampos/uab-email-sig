Instructions for Adding Your Signature

Windows — Outlook: Copy then File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Paste with “Merge Formatting.”

Mac — Outlook & Mail App: Copy then paste into your signature settings.

iOS — Mail App: Copy then Settings → Mail → Signature. Paste there.
